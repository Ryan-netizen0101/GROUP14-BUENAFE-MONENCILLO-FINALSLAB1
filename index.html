How to Use

Follow these steps one by one. Pop-up boxes will appear on your screen, so read each one carefully.

  1. Press the START button Find the big red START button and click it. A welcome box will pop up. Click OK to move on.
  2. Type your name A box will ask for your name. Type your name using letters only, then click OK. Do not leave it empty or use numbers and symbols.
  3. Type your score The next box will ask for your score. Type a number from 1 to 100, then click OK. Do not use letters, zero, negative numbers, or numbers above 100.
  4. Confirm to continue A last box will ask if you want to continue. Click OK to see your result, or Cancel to stop.
  5. Read your result Your name, score, and remark (Excellent, Passed, or Failed) will show in the Result box. If you made a mistake, you will see a message telling you what to fix.
  6. Try again Want to check another score? Just press START again.
